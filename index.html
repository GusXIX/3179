Explanation of Yelp Price Rating

$ (Inexpensive): Businesses with one dollar sign are typically the most budget-friendly.
$$ (Moderate): These are mid-range priced options.
$$$ (Expensive): Businesses in this category are pricier.
$$$$ (Very Expensive): This represents the highest price tier.
